# Privacy notice

*AKS: From Zero to Secure Production* is a free community series run by volunteers and organised by Sathpal ("we"). This notice explains what personal data the series uses, why, and for how long.

## What we collect and why

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email and your answers on the registration form | You register for a session | To send your join link and reminders, to invite you to the series' Team if you ask, and to track completion |
| Guest account (name and email) in our Microsoft 365 organisation | Only if you opt in to the Team | So you can open the session channels, materials and recordings |
| Attendance report (join and leave times) | You attend live | Completion tracking |
| Your Q&A questions, with your display name | You ask during a session | To answer them, during or after the session |
| Completion form and capstone link | You apply for completion | To review your capstone and issue a certificate |

## Recordings

Every session is **recorded and published** on YouTube and in the series' Team. Attendee microphones and cameras are off and attendee names are hidden, so recordings show the presenters and their screens. Questions from the Q&A panel may be read aloud by first name.

## Where it's stored

Registrations, guest accounts, attendance and Q&A live in our Microsoft 365 organisation (Microsoft Teams and SharePoint). Recordings are on YouTube. Anything you post in GitHub Discussions or issues is public, under GitHub's terms.

## What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data, add you to mailing lists unrelated to the series, or pass your details to sponsors or employers.

## How long we keep it

We delete registration data, attendance reports, Q&A exports and guest accounts by **31 December 2027**. Recordings stay published as course material.

## Your choices

You can leave the Team at any time. You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 allows, by writing to the organisers at the contact address in the Team's `Start Here` channel.
