# Code of conduct

This series follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in live sessions, the Team, GitHub Discussions, issues and anywhere else the series meets.

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and help others learn. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Rules for live sessions and channels

- Keep the chat and the Q&A on topic. No selling, recruiting, promotion or crypto, and no links to unrelated content.
- Don't share other participants' personal details, or screenshots of them.
- Never post secrets: keys, passwords, tokens, kubeconfig files or connection strings.
- Re-uploading recordings elsewhere is not allowed. Link to the official playlist instead.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the organisers; the contact address is in the Team's `Start Here` channel. Moderators may remove messages, mute participants, or remove someone from a session, the Team or the repository, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
