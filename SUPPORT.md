# Getting help

Volunteers run this series and around 500 people take part, so help works best in the open, where everyone can learn from the answer.

| Where | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Teams **Q&A** panel, during a live session | Questions about what's on screen now. Moderators answer, or pass them to the instructor |
| [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/sathpal/aks-zero-to-secure/discussions) | Lab questions after a session, assignment answers (one category per session), capstone help, show-and-tell |
| Teams `Help and Discussion` channel | The same, for members of the series' Team |
| [Lab-help issue](https://github.com/sathpal/aks-zero-to-secure/issues/new?template=lab-help.yml) | A step in the materials is wrong or out of date |

## How to ask so you get an answer quickly

1. Say which session, which part and which step.
2. Paste the **exact command** and its **full output** as text, not a screenshot.
3. Say your OS (macOS, Linux or WSL2) and your subscription type (free trial or pay-as-you-go).
4. Say what you already tried.

**Never post** subscription IDs together with keys, passwords, kubeconfig files, access tokens or connection strings. If you post one by mistake, delete the post and rotate the credential.

Please don't direct-message the instructor or moderators with lab questions. Answers in the open help the next person too. Expect best-effort replies, usually within a few days.
